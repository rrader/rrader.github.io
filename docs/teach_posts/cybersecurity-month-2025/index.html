Cybersecurity Awareness Month 2025

🌐 Other languages:🇺🇦 Українська

About Cybersecurity Awareness Month

Cybersecurity Awareness Month is a global initiative dedicated to raising awareness about the importance of security in the digital world. In October 2025, we joined this initiative by offering a series of interactive activities and quizzes designed to help students better understand the fundamentals of digital safety, personal data protection, and secure web browsing.

Acknowledgements

These interactive activities are based on the course materials from Digital Skills: Security and Safety by Charles Sturt University (Australia). The materials were translated and adapted into Ukrainian for our classes.

Interactive Modules & Quizzes

1. Protecting Your Personal Information

2. Strong and Safe Passwords

3. Facebook Login for Third-Party Platforms

4. Internet Essentials: The Internet and the Web

5. Digital Wellbeing and Privacy: Attention Distractions

6. Quiz: Internet Essentials

7. Quiz: Social Media Overview

8. Quiz: Test Your Knowledge

9. Quiz: Digital Security
