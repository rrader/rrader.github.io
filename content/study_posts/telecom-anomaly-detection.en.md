A new issue of the KPI Bulletin on Computer Engineering was published featuring my research paper (co-authored with P. N. Malyukov):

> **Malyukov P. N., Rader R. I.** *Anomaly Detection System for Telephone Network Subscribers.*  
> Bulletin of NTUU "KPI". Informatics, Control and Computer Science: Collection of Scientific Papers. – Kyiv: Vek+, 2013. – No. 59. – pp. 106–110.

### Abstract

An algorithm and system for detecting anomalous behavior of subscribers considering group behavior changes are proposed. A prototype and user behavior simulator for analyzing the algorithm were developed. The algorithm performance was analyzed in three modes: single-user, multi-user without trend consideration, and multi-user with trend consideration.
